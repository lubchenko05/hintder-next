Hugo had a habit he wasn't proud of. Somebody would mention their work, or a band, or a book, and he'd nod. *Yeah, mad.* *Ah, class.* He'd absorb just enough to keep up and steer the conversation somewhere he sounded better.

It worked, in the sense that nobody caught him. It also meant that at 31, he'd never once had the kind of conversation where the other person forgot to be self-conscious.

## Two days of nodding

Saoirse, 29, mixed live sound at venues around Dublin. Her messages came in late and slightly wired — load-ins, soundchecks, a drummer who kept moving his own mics. Hugo understood almost none of it.

So he did what he always did. *Sounds intense.* *Nightmare.* Two days of that, and the thread was going exactly the way his threads always went: pleasant, thinning out, headed nowhere.

Then she mentioned a wedge mix, and something in him gave up. Instead of the usual nod, he typed the true thing:

> "Okay, honest answer: I have no idea what a wedge mix is. I've been nodding along for two days."

Not clever. Not charming. Just true, with a small confession attached.

## What honesty actually unlocked

She replied in about forty seconds, and then she kept replying. Wedges, in-ears, why a room sounds different once four hundred bodies are in it. She sent a voice note. She apologised for the voice note and then sent another one.

Somewhere in there she wrote: *most people just say "cool" and change the subject.*

That's the part Hugo thinks about. He hadn't given her anything impressive. He'd given her permission to talk about the thing she loved to someone who actually wanted the answer — which, it turns out, almost nobody does.

## The lesson he'd have paid money for at 25

Being interesting is exhausting and it doesn't work. Being curious costs nothing and it works nearly every time. The line that changed it wasn't a good line — it was an honest one, sent at the moment he'd normally have bluffed.

That's the kind of line hintder is good at finding: not a clever performance, just the true thing you were already thinking, phrased so it lands. It drafts it. You still have to be the one brave enough to hit send.

Hugo hit send. Two years on, she's still explaining things to him, and he's still asking.
