Jonah was, by every measure, a good texter. Quick, warm, funny when it counted. He'd get a message and fire back something clever, and then he'd sit there watching three dots appear, disappear, and never come back.

For a long time he blamed the apps. Too many options, everyone flaky, nobody wanting anything real. What he didn't see was the shape of his own half of every conversation: she'd offer something, he'd react to it, and the thread would land on his reply like a ball rolling to a stop. He always took the last word. He just never handed anything back.

## The night he actually asked

Tessa mentioned, almost in passing, that she'd started playing cello again after five years away. The old Jonah had a "that's cool" loaded and ready — the exact kind of reply that closes a door while pretending to hold it open.

This time he stopped and did the one thing he never did. He got curious out loud.

> "Okay, real question — what made you pick it back up after all that time?"

It wasn't clever. It wasn't a line. It was just a door left open — a question only she could answer. And she walked right through it, and told him about her grandmother, and the case that sat unopened for a year. The conversation didn't land on his reply this time. It kept going, because for once he'd given her somewhere to go.

## What actually changed

That's the whole turn of the story. Not a smoother opener, not better banter — Jonah already had all of that. What he was missing was the smallest, most generous move in any conversation: asking the other person something real and then getting out of the way.

A good question at the right moment is exactly the kind of thing hintder is built to hand you when your own instinct is to reach for "that's cool." It drafts the line; Jonah's the one who sent it, and kept sending them, all night at a jazz club she picked, and at a recital four months later where he sat front row and just watched.

A year in, she still practices in the corner of their apartment while he cooks. He still, mostly, remembers to ask the question back.
