You had momentum. She was sending two messages back for every one of yours. You sent something. Then it slowed. Then it stopped.

This is not random. Most quiet-out patterns map to one of five causes — and four of them are recoverable.

## Reason 1: You answered before reading

The most common kill is **a reply that doesn't engage with what she just said.** She asks "what's your weirdest skill" — you reply with "haha, you?". She thinks she's talking to a wall.

Fix: re-read her last message *out loud* before composing yours. If your reply could plausibly follow any random message, rewrite.

## Reason 2: You over-corrected after a joke

She made a small dig. You doubled down with three jokes in a row, trying to keep the energy. She wanted **back-and-forth**, not a stand-up set. Comedy fatigues fast in text.

Fix: one bit per turn. Match her cadence. If her last was 2 sentences, yours is 2 sentences.

## Reason 3: You asked for the meeting too early

This one is brutal: she was actually interested. You felt the heat and asked "want to grab a drink this week?" before round 4. She got the ask-pressure feeling and **invented a reason to be unavailable** — which means she's now lying to you, which means she's looking for an exit.

Fix: ask only when the readiness signal is real (multiple personal questions back, jokes that build on yours, a question about *you*).

## Reason 4: You went serious too fast

A 2-line burst about how meaningful something is, or a "real talk" moment, when the thread had been light. She wasn't in that mode yet.

Fix: depth comes through callbacks, not declarations. Reference something she said three messages ago, instead of confessing something.

## Reason 5: She's actually not interested

This is the one that's not recoverable. You'll know by the signal: replies that are one-word, no questions back, response times growing. **If you sent two messages without a question between them and she didn't bite — close the thread cleanly.**

## The three recovery scripts

### Script A: re-engage after 24h silence (warming was real)

> "Realised I never followed up on the [her thing]. Did you end up [doing that thing]?"

Specific. Picks up where you left off. No grovelling.

### Script B: pattern interrupt after a flat reply

> "Okay different question — [thing pulled from her bio you haven't asked yet]?"

Resets the mode. Shows you're not boxed into the current line.

### Script C: clean close (you tried, she's gone)

> "Sounds like you got busy — no worries. Enjoy your week."

No drama, no second attempt later. Burn the thread, save your time.

## The hard rule

**One recovery attempt. That's it.** If she ghosts after the recovery line, she's gone. Sending a third "everything okay?" is the move that confirms what she suspected.
