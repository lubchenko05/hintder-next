Getting a like with a comment is the app handing you a cheat code. She saw your profile, picked one specific thing, and typed words about it. She has done the hardest part of the opener — finding the hook — and left it sitting there for you. And yet the standard reply is "hey thanks haha," which is how you take a layup and dribble it off your own foot.

The rule for all of these: **answer her actual comment, not the fact that she commented.** She didn't send you a "hey." She sent you a specific reaction to a specific thing. Reward the specificity by matching it. Here are six comment types you'll see and how to turn each one into a thread instead of a thank-you.

## 1. The genuine question ("Wait, where is this hike?")

She asked something answerable. This is the friendliest possible open — just don't answer it like a park ranger. Give the fact, then add a hook she can grab.

> that's Runyon, about twenty minutes out. i go early specifically so i can pretend i'm a morning person for exactly one hour a week. you a trail person or a "brunch is my cardio" person?

You answered, added a bit of personality, and handed her a two-option question. Same engine as [guessing instead of asking](/guides/guess-instead-of-asking) — you give her something to react to, not a blank.

## 2. The compliment ("Okay the dog photo got me")

She's being warm and a little vulnerable — she led with a compliment, which takes nerve. Don't just absorb it with "haha thanks." Deflect it playfully onto the subject and keep the ball moving.

> he knows exactly what he's doing, that's his whole personality. he's the reason i get matches and he takes zero percent of the credit. do you have a resident animal or are you between pets

If [your photo has a dog](/guides/her-photo-has-a-dog), the dog is the conversation — lean all the way in.

## 3. The tease ("Bold to claim you can cook")

This is the best one you can get. She's flirting by poking at you. If you get defensive or over-explain, you kill it. Push back, warmly, and raise the stakes.

> big words from someone who hasn't tasted the pasta. that's a check you might have to cash. what's your signature dish, or is it a reservation

You met her energy instead of shrinking from it — the exact move in [teasing without being a jerk](/guides/teasing-without-being-a-jerk). She started the bit; you're now playing it together.

## 4. The one-word / emoji comment ("😂" or "lol same")

Low effort, but not a rejection — she still spent a like on you. Treat it like a [one-word answer](/guides/replying-to-one-word-answers): don't match her brevity, add the words she didn't.

> "lol same" is doing a lot of heavy lifting there. same as in you also once got aggressively lost in a Costco, or same as in you're just being nice

Naming the vagueness and being a little cheeky about it forces a real answer without any pressure.

## 5. The niche reference ("Is that a Criterion shelf behind you??")

She spotted something specific and nerdy in your photo. This is gold — she's telling you exactly what she's into. Reward the catch and go deeper on it.

> a woman who clocks the Criterion spines is a genuine threat to my free time. yes, and it's aggressively curated to look smarter than i am. what's the one you'd defend to the death

You validated her observation and opened a lane she clearly wants to drive in. Shared-taste threads run for days if you don't smother them.

## 6. The vague opener ("Hey you seem cool")

The weakest comment she can leave — basically a "hey" with a costume on. Don't punish her for it, but don't accept the low bar either. Give the thread the specificity she didn't.

> "seem" is carrying some suspicion in that sentence and i respect the caution. i'm cool in a "will send you an unprompted 4am fact about octopuses" way. adjust your expectations accordingly

You took nothing and made something. That's the same skill as [opening when her profile is basically empty](/guides/her-profile-is-basically-empty) — you supply the personality the exchange is missing.

Across all six, the move is identical: **treat her comment as the first line of a conversation you're already in, not a notification to acknowledge.** She skipped the awkward cold open for you. The only way to fumble it is to reply with less energy than she brought.

If you're staring at her comment blanking on how to match it — especially the teasing kind, where the wrong tone reads as either wounded or arrogant — that's a fair spot to get a second read. Drop a screenshot into [hintder](/) and it'll draft a few replies in the tone you pick, so you send something with a hook instead of "hey thanks." You still choose the line and hit send; first three are free.

She already told you what she noticed. Answer *that*, and you're two messages into a real conversation while everyone else is still typing "thanks 😊."
